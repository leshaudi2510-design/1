Privacy

The short version: there are no accounts, no sign-up forms and no tracking at Pixel Crown Club.

What we store, and where

The club saves these things in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • your Crowns balance,
  • the time you last claimed your allowance,
  • that you confirmed you are 18 or over.

This information never leaves your device and we can't see it. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Membership and sound settings

Your membership tier, game statistics, trophies, card number and sound preference are also kept in your browser's local storage. The card number is random and is not linked to you.

Analytics, only if you agree

If analytics is switched on for the club, you'll be asked first. Only if you choose “Allow analytics” do we load Google Analytics 4 and/or Yandex Metrica, which set cookies to measure visits and which tables are played. Your choice is remembered in local storage, and you can reset it by clearing this site's data.

We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we never collect your name, email address or payment details.

Offline app

If you install the club as an app, your browser keeps a copy of the site's files so it works offline. This cache holds no personal data.

Third parties

Fonts are served from our own site. The site is delivered by a web host, which, like any website request, receives your IP address and basic browser information to send you the files.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too.
