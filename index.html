      Before you play

      Is this real gambling?

      No. Pixel Crown Club is a social casino game. You never pay to play, and nothing you win can be turned into money, goods or prizes.

      Can I buy more Crowns?

      No. Crowns are only given away: 4,096 when you arrive, 1,024 every 8 hours, and a refill to 1,024 whenever you drop below 16.

      Where is my balance saved?

      In your browser's local storage on this device. There is no account. Clearing your browser's site data, or playing on another device, starts you again with 4,096 Crowns.

      Are the games fair?

      Every spin, ball and card is drawn with your browser's cryptographic random number generator. Crown Reels returns about 94.5% of stakes over the long run. Royal Wheel is a standard European single-zero wheel. Club 21 deals from a six-deck shoe.

      How do membership tiers work?

      Every Crown you stake counts toward your tier: Silver at 4,096, Gold at 32,768, Platinum at 131,072 and Crown at 524,288. Tiers change your card's finish and add larger chips. They never cost anything and have no cash value.

      Can I install the club as an app?

      Yes. On Android and desktop Chrome or Edge, use the install button in the footer or your browser menu. On iPhone, open the page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The club keeps working offline once installed.

      Why is the club 18+?

      Casino-style games are made for adults, even when no money is involved. Please don't let anyone under 18 play on your device.
